Gameplay & Game Modes

The game offers a variety of ways to play. Career Mode acts as the backbone, featuring over 800 events across several seasons. Multiplayer Mode allows players to climb the ranks from Bronze to Legend league in real-time races. Special Events and Grand Prix are limited-time modes where players can win the blueprints for some of the rarest cars in the game.

The Science of Speed: Nitro

Nitro is the lifeblood of Asphalt Legends. There are four types of Nitro boosts: Level 1 (Yellow), Perfect Nitro (Blue), Level 2 (Orange), and the powerful Nitro Shockwave (Purple). Choosing the right nitro at the right time—such as using Shockwave to blast through opponents or Blue Nitro to maintain speed over rough terrain—is the difference between first and last place.

Car Classes and Tiers

Vehicles are categorized from Class D (starters) to Class S (the fastest cars in the world). Each car has a Rank, which can be increased by applying upgrades to the Top Speed, Acceleration, Handling, and Nitro stats. Additionally, Import Parts can be installed to fine-tune a car's performance for high-level competitive play.

Iconic Global Locations

The game takes racers across the globe, from the neon-lit streets of Osaka and the scenic cliffs of the Himalayas to the bustling metropolis of New York. Each track is filled with shortcuts, ramps for aerial stunts, and destructible environments that can be used to gain an edge over the competition.

Clubs and Social Play

Racing is better with friends. By joining a Club, players can combine their Reputation Points to unlock massive rewards for everyone in the group. Clubs also provide a chat interface for sharing strategy and organizing "Club Races" to practice on specific tracks without affecting your multiplayer rank.
